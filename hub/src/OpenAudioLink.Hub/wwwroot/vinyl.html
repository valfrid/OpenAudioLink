The level is read from the node's capture stage — its ADC, or its microphone — and updates every few seconds, so you can tell a wiring fault from a quiet passage before you walk back to the speaker. On a turntable, one channel silent while the other is not is nearly always the cable. On a microphone it is simply how one capsule works, and the reading above says which slot it landed in.
